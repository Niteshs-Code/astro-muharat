ASTRO MUHURAT

The Right Time Can Change Everything.

Muhurat is highly significant. Time is changing every moment. Some times are auspicious, while others are inauspicious.

Actions performed during auspicious times yield favorable results, whereas those done in inauspicious times may bring sorrow, harm, suffering, accidents and more.

Our sages discovered the science of Muhurat for human welfare by calculating time and sensing the movement of planets and constellations.

Through this science, when we begin any task at an auspicious time, we seek favorable results. This calculation varies for every individual, because every person is born under different planetary combinations.

UNDERSTANDING MUHURAT

What is a Muhurat?

A Muhurat means a time that provides you with favorable results.

Every Moment Has Its Significance.

Every day has auspicious and inauspicious periods. Different Muhurats are determined for different tasks and different people.

Some times may be auspicious for one person but inauspicious for another. Each person is different, so the Muhurat for each individual is also different.

There is a separate Muhurat for every task. Muhurats for buying a vehicle, starting a business, beginning house construction, laying a foundation or entering a new house can all be different.

To determine the Muhurat, astrologers study planetary positions and constellations, understand Rahukaal and calculate the auspicious moment according to the individual.
